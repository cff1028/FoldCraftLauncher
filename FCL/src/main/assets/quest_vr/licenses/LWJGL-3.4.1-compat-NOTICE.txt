Based on QuestCraftPlusPlus/lwjgl3 ec0bbebe4379f59fe956c9a79e44d3b09837b134
(OpenXR, including Android extensions) and LWJGL OpenGL ES 3.3.3 sources.
Adapted Callback.Descriptor, absolute scalar memory operations, and
StructBuffer view factories for the packaged LWJGL 3.4.1 core.
Original Android OpenXR APIs and GLES JNI signatures are retained.
These are FCL compatibility builds, not official LWJGL 3.4.1 modules.
Corresponding source and rebuild script: third-party/quest-vr/lwjgl341-compat
in the FCL Quest VR source distribution.
